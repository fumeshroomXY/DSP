# Digital Signal Processing (DSP)
DSP is the technique of analyzing, modifying, and manipulating signals after they have been converted into digital data.

For example, when a microphone records sound:

- The sound wave is an analog signal.
- It is sampled and converted into **digital values**.
- DSP algorithms process those values.
- The processed result is output as cleaner audio, frequency information, etc.

## Common DSP Operations
### Filtering
Remove unwanted frequencies or noise.

Example:

- Noise cancellation in headphones
- Removing electrical interference from sensor data


### FFT (Fast Fourier Transform)

Converts a signal from the time domain to the **frequency domain**.

Example:

- Finding which frequencies exist in a sound signal


### Compression

Reduce data size.

Example:

- MP3 audio
- JPEG images


### Detection and Analysis

Extract useful information from signals.

Example:

- Speech recognition
- Vibration fault diagnosis
